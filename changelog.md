# Changelog

## Version 18.0 (2026-01-04)

### New Features

*   **Initial Release:** Module launch for zero sales restriction and administration.
*   **Stock Validation:** Added restriction in Sales to prevent confirmation if there is no stock on hand.
*   **Validations:** Added restrictions to prevent Invoices (Customer/Vendor), Credit Notes, and POS orders with 0 amount.
*   **Duplicate Prevention:** Added policy to prevent duplicate product lines in Sales and Invoices.
