Prevent Zero Sales

Strict control of amounts and stock in Sales, Invoicing and POS

General Description

Ensure the integrity of your sales operations

This module adds an essential security layer to prevent common errors in the sales process. It strictly prevents customer invoices, vendor bills, credit notes, and point of sale orders from being processed with a total amount of 0. Additionally, it verifies stock availability before confirming sale orders.

Main Features

Invoice Validation

Prevents confirmation of invoices (Customer/Vendor) and credit notes if the total amount is 0, avoiding erroneous accounting entries.

Stock Control

Blocks the confirmation of Sale Orders if there is not enough stock on hand (qty_available), preventing sales without inventory.

POS Security

Restricts the creation of orders in the Point of Sale if the total amount is 0, ensuring that every transaction has value.

Duplicate Prevention

Prevents adding the same product multiple times in Sales Orders and Invoices to ensure clean and correct data entry.
